Six bush variants were selected from the publisher GLB models and converted to
binary FBX with their flat colour materials. Static meshes were joined and pivots
centred at the bottom. These models are small at native scale; try a uniform
Unity Transform Scale of (3, 3, 3) for roadside bushes, then adjust to your layout.
